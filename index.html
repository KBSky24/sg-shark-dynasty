00

A year in this league

The whole thing on one strip. Blue months are the season you already know; gold months are what makes it dynasty.

  • You draft once, ever. After that your team is yours until you trade or cut a player.
  • The season is the 9-cat league you already play, minus the chores. One lineup a week. One optional bid a week. Trades whenever.
  • Late March: the final ends. The rest of the buy-in comes in and prize money is paid. Your roster rolls over untouched.
  • May: the teams that missed the playoffs draw for the first pick.
  • June: you draft rookies. Three rounds. Picks are yours to use or trade, three years out.
  • September: the league votes on its rules, everyone cuts down, and the whole free-agent pool goes to one auction where the bottom team has the biggest budget.
  • October: it starts again.
  • A down season is a building season. Your young players are a year closer, your pick is high, your auction budget is the biggest in the league.
  • A great roster doesn't get to stand still. The champion picks last, shops with the smallest budget, and has to make room for every new player he wants.
01

Three principles

Every rule below traces back to one of these. When a rule change is argued in year two, argue the principle first.

Scarcity is the game

A dynasty league is only interesting if a good player is hard to replace. Roster limits, the pickup cap and the once-a-year auction exist to manufacture scarcity.

Few decisions, each one heavy

Remove the low-value chores (daily lineups, free-agent churn). Keep the high-value ones: draft, trade, one weekly lineup. Five minutes a week; three good evenings a year.

Nobody leaves

Dynasty leagues die from owner churn, not bad rules. The lottery, the inverse auction budget, the annual cutdown and a wide playoff field keep every bottom team eighteen months from contending, and it knows it.

Rule 2.4 · Taxi squadWhere rookies live

  • 3 slots for players in their first or second NBA season. They don't count against the 15 and can't be started.
  • A player leaves taxi at any time and can never return. Taxi players can be traded.
  • After his second season a player is promoted or released at the September cutdown.

Rule 2.5 · LineupsWeekly lock, no exceptions

  • Lineups lock at the first tip on Monday (ET), Tuesday morning SGT. No daily setting.
  • No injury exception. If your starter is hurt on Tuesday, you're unlucky; that's fantasy.

Rule 2.6 · Injured reserveOfficial injuries only

  • Only for players officially Out or Out For Season.
  • Off IR within 7 days of clearing, or the roster is illegal.
  • IR moves take effect at the next weekly lock.

Season structure 2026-27

  • Regular season: 18 scoring weeks from opening night (~Oct 20). Everyone plays everyone once and seven opponents twice; Fantrax builds the schedule.
  • The All-Star week (mid-Feb) is merged with the week after it. Regular season ends ~Feb 28.
  • Playoffs Mar 1–28: top 8, no byes. Rounds are one week; the final is two weeks.
  • Third-place game between the semi-final losers, over the final's two weeks. It decides 3rd-place money.
  • Hard stop before the NBA's final week, so resting stars don't decide the title.
  • "Finish" everywhere in this charter: champion, runner-up, 3rd-place game winner and loser, quarter-final losers by record, then teams 9–12 by record.
03

Startup draft

Once, ever. Everyone starts from zero; the whole NBA is in the pool, this year's rookies included.

Rule 3.1 · Format18 rounds, snake, third-round reversal

  • 18 rounds (15 active + 3 taxi), 216 picks.
  • Order runs 1→12, 12→1, 12→1 again, then a normal snake.
  • Draft order is randomised by Fantrax when the draft is set up.
  • Drafted NBA rookies may go to taxi. Anyone with more than 15 non-rookies cuts down before opening night.
Why the reversalThe 1.01 in dynasty is worth eight seasons, not one. The last slot gets picks 12, 13 and 25, which evens the opening gap.

Rule 3.3 · 2026 startupSlow draft, starts on creation

  • Starts as soon as the league exists on Fantrax (target Oct 5).
  • 1 hour per pick, frozen overnight 1am–9am SGT.
  • Worst case, every pick uses its full hour: 216 picks in 13½ days, done Oct 19, the day before tip-off.
  • Pace check Oct 12: if the draft hasn't reached round 10, the clock drops to 30 minutes.
  • A timed-out pick is auto-drafted from your queue or Fantrax's rankings. Set your queue. Trades are open during the draft.

Rookie draft and lottery

How new players enter, how the order is set, and how a bottom team climbs back.

Rule 5.1 · Rookie draft3 rounds, 36 picks, straight order

  • Eligible: players entering their first NBA season (that year's draft class, undrafted rookies, first-year arrivals from overseas).
  • Straight order: the same order every round. "A 2028 first" is one well-understood asset.
  • Starts 72 hours after the second night of the NBA draft. 8-hour clock, frozen overnight SGT. Live pick trading throughout.
  • Undrafted rookies go to the Frenzy pool. Rosters may exceed the limit until the September cutdown.
Draft orderPicks 1–4: drawn in the lottery. Picks 5–12: playoff teams in reverse finish; the champion picks 12th.

Rule 5.3 · Lineup integrityThe only penalty in the charter Voted

  • A team with an empty starting slot in four or more scoring weeks forfeits its lottery balls and picks last among the non-playoff teams.
  • Waived for a team in receivership (Rule 10.5).
WhyEmpty starting slots are the one thing that ruins other people's seasons.

06

Trading

The main event. Rules here enable trades; they don't police them.

Rule 6.1 · What can be tradedFour assets, nothing else

PlayersActive, taxi or IR.
Rookie picksIn the next three drafts.
In-season dollarsDuring the season.
Frenzy budgetYes, the Frenzy budget is tradeable, from the day budgets are announced (the day after the final) until the Frenzy closes.

No future considerations, no side deals, no "I'll owe you one." Real money is never part of a trade.

Why Frenzy dollars tradeIt's the NBA's "cap space for picks" deal. A rebuilding team with a big budget and nothing to spend it on sells part of it to a contender for a pick.

Rules 6.2–6.4Vetoes, windows, picks

  • No vetoes for value. Collusion review only: commissioner and vice-commissioner, unanimous, within 24 hours. If one is a party, the other rules alone.
  • Windows: trading closes at the NBA trade deadline and reopens the day after the league final. Open all offseason, including live during the rookie draft and through the Frenzy.
  • Picks stay picks. A traded pick is the pick itself, wherever it lands. Fantrax tracks it; the commissioner keeps a ledger too.
  • Trades go through on commissioner review; there is no league vote.
Why no vetoesBad trades are self-correcting in dynasty. Whoever traded a 23-year-old star for a 33-year-old one lives with it for six years.

Free agency

One blind bid a week in season. One auction a year in September. No streaming, ever. All bidding is in fantasy dollars.

Rule 7.1 · In-seasonOne blind bid, Sunday night Voted

  • No open free agency. Every acquisition goes through one blind bid processed Sunday 11pm ET (Monday 11am–12pm SGT).
  • Season budget $100, minimum bid $1, ties to the worse record.
  • You pay what you bid. (No proxy bidding, where the winner pays $1 over the second-highest bid.)
  • Dropped players go straight into the bid pool. No free pickups between runs.
  • Bidding runs through the playoffs; unspent dollars vanish when the final ends.
  • Hard cap: 12 acquisitions per team per season.
Why a capWith weekly lineups the wire only matters if someone streams the bench for four-game weeks. That's the grind this league exists to kill.

Rule 7.2 · CutdownSept 10 · at or below the limit

  • Every team must be at or below 15 active + 3 taxi. IR only for players still officially out.
  • How far below is your choice. Want open spots for the Frenzy? Cut deeper.
  • Aged-out taxi players are promoted or released here. Cuts go to the pool.
Why after the voteA roster-limit change passed in September is applied once, here, and the Frenzy fills to the new size.

Rule 7.3 · FA Frenzy~Sept 20 · one blind multi-round auction

  • After the cutdown and any expansion draft, every cut, every undrafted rookie and every unrostered NBA player goes into one pool.
  • A winning bid needs an open roster spot or a named drop. Tied bids go to the worse finish.
  • Budgets by last season's finish. Announced the day after the final; tradeable from then. Expansion teams get the top budget.
Rule 7.4 · No single bid above half your budget. The champion can put at most $30 on one player; a mid-table team with $120 can go to $60; the bottom team to $85. The ladder sets how many players you can afford; this rule stops a full roster from buying the best one anyway.
Rule 7.5 · Budgets are separateFrenzy dollars and in-season dollars never mix, and unspent Frenzy money doesn't carry over. The commissioner sets Frenzy budgets by hand before the auction and resets everyone to $100 before tip-off.

08

Why a superteam decays

No rule set prevents one. These make holding a great roster cost something every year, and give the teams below an automatic supply of talent.

Annual cutdown

A stacked team can't add without releasing, and every release goes into a pool the bottom team bids on with the most money.

Inverse budgets + half-bid cap

The bottom team has nearly 3× the champion's money and can always outbid him for any one player.

Weighted lottery

Non-playoff teams get picks 1–4. The champion picks last.

Three-draft horizon

Nobody can mortgage more than three years of a franchise.

Contracts and cap Year 3+

Native on Fantrax Premium. Not in year one: it doubles offseason admin. Revisit in September 2029, or when someone complains a team is too good.

Expansion

The league is built to grow. New teams are voted in during the September window and play that October.

The September sequence in an expansion year

VoteSept 1–7Three-quarters of owners. Each incoming owner named before the vote. Any number of teams; even preferred.
CutdownSept 10Everyone at or below the limit.
ProtectSept 12Each existing team protects 60% of its active roster, rounded up: 9 of 15. Taxi auto-protected; IR counts as active.
Expansion draft~Sept 15 · liveNew teams snake-draft 8 players each (coin flip, A-B-B-A). No existing team loses more than ⌈8 × new ÷ existing⌉: 2 at 12→14.
Frenzy~Sept 20Expansion teams get the top budget. Anyone still over the new limit cuts down first.
Tip-offOctoberNew teams own all their future picks and enter the next lottery on their own record.

Protect 9, lose at most 2. How?

  • Protected (9): untouchable.
  • Exposed (6): the new teams may pick from these.
  • Per-team cap (2): the most any one team loses.

The protection count decides which players you lose: the best of your six exposed. Exposing six is not a risk of losing six.

Worked example · 12 → 14Two new teams need 16 players. Twelve teams expose 72; 16 are taken, roughly each roster's 10th–12th men; the other 56 stay put. Four teams lose 2, eight lose 1.

What a new team looks like

  • 8 rotation players from the expansion draft.
  • The biggest budget in the Frenzy.
  • A full set of its own picks.

Target: mid-table in year one. Competitive, not a champion.

PrecedentCharlotte 2004 protected 8 of 15. Vegas 2017 protected about 11 of 23. Verified Fantrax adds teams at renewal with existing rosters untouched.

The buy-in, the prizes, the penalty for walking out, and how the league outlives any one of us.

When a team is left without an owner

ReceivershipRule 10.5 · 2 weeks silentTwo scoring weeks with no legal lineup and no reply in the chat. The commissioner sets a legal lineup each Monday; trading and bidding frozen; Rule 5.3 waived.
Offered onafter 4 weeks, or on exitTo the reserve list first, then publicly. An owner who leaves without notice may not trade after announcing.

Contractionno owner when the window closesPlayers to remaining teams by reverse-finish snake, max two each, rest to the Frenzy. Its own future picks void; picks it traded away become compensatory picks; picks it held return to their teams.
BannedRedistributing an orphan's players among existing owners, except on contraction. Letting a friend run two teams: collusion risk, no incentive to make the orphan good.

Rule 10.4 · AmendmentsSeptember only

  • Proposed only in the Sept 1–7 window.
  • Pass on three-quarters of owners, rounded up: 9 of 12.
  • Take effect when the window closes. Nothing changes at any other time, even unanimously.
  • Expansion goes through the same window and threshold.

Rule 10.8 · ContinuityThe league outlives any one of us

  • A reserve list of would-be owners with first refusal on orphans and expansion seats.
  • The vice-commissioner, Timothy Kong, is a Fantrax co-commissioner and succeeds automatically.
  • Daily backups (Premium) on. The pick ledger and protection lists also live in a shared document.
  • One group chat is league infrastructure: trades announced, votes held, lottery seeds posted.
